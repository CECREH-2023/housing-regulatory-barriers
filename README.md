# Housing Regulatory Barriers Prototype

This prototype brings together public permitting, housing, socioeconomic, hazard, and regulatory-text data to explore how housing restrictions could be measured. Its outputs describe available indicators and source coverage.

## Results and interpretation

The prototype links public permitting, housing composition, socioeconomic and hazard measures with regulatory-text counts. Its reference summaries show the available descriptive indicators and their coverage for developing a housing-regulation study.

This version does **not estimate a causal effect of regulatory barriers**. Dated policy changes, comparable outcome measures, and a separate identification design are required for that question. Text frequency alone does not establish a binding restriction, implementation practice, or enforcement intensity.
